#### Treatment-encoding sensitivity surface (sixteen specifications)

Within-country two-way (country and year) fixed effects with country-clustered standard errors. Controls: log GDP per capita; primary pupil-teacher ratio; education expenditure as percent of GDP; the single Government Effectiveness indicator (WGI). Primary window 2010–2020. Treatment enters as the log of one plus the indicated transform.

Transform abbreviations: raw = annual disbursement (no transform); 1-yr lag = the t−1 value; 3-yr MA = the mean of t−2, t−1, t (trailing-inclusive); 3-yr MA strictly past = the mean of t−3, t−2, t−1 (primary specification).
