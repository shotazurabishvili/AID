# Lit Note: Glewwe & Muralidharan (2016)

**Full cite (APA 7):** Glewwe, P., & Muralidharan, K. (2016). Improving education outcomes in developing countries: Evidence, knowledge gaps, and policy implications. In E. A. Hanushek, S. Machin, & L. Woessmann (Eds.), *Handbook of the Economics of Education* (Vol. 5, pp. 653–743). Elsevier.

## Argument
Comprehensive review of what works in education in developing countries. Effective interventions cluster in specific categories: pedagogical interventions (targeted instruction, tracking by skill), accountability (information to parents), and selective school inputs (textbooks for poor schools). Mass input interventions (more teachers, more books, more spending generally) often fail to move learning.

## Method
- Systematic review of RCTs and quasi-experimental studies
- Categorization of interventions by mechanism
- Meta-analysis where data permits
