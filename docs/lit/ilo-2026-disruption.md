# Lit Note: International Labour Organization & World Bank (2026)

**Full cite (APA 7):** International Labour Organization & World Bank. (2026, March). *Disruption without dividend? How the digital divide and task differences split GenAI's global impact*. International Labour Organization and World Bank. (Background study for the *World Development Report 2026*.)
**DOI / URL:** https://www.ilo.org/publications/disruption-without-dividend-how-digital-divide-and-task-differences-split

## Argument
Generative AI is set to reshape labor markets globally, but with uneven impacts across countries. In developing economies, *disruption may materialize faster than productivity gains* because workers vulnerable to automation typically retain enough internet access to experience displacement, while workers who could benefit from GenAI augmentation face digital-infrastructure gaps that prevent them from realizing the productivity gains. The headline figure: across the 135 countries with detailed data, ≈441.8 million jobs fall into augmentation-oriented exposure gradients, of which ≈66.9 million lack the internet access needed to realize the augmentation potential.

## Method
- 135-country labor-market exposure analysis covering ≈two-thirds of global employment
- Occupational decomposition: displacement-exposed vs augmentation-exposed task profiles
- Cross-country digital-divide indicators × exposure profiles
- Joint ILO–World Bank construction; background study for *World Development Report 2026*
