# Lit Note: Altinok, Angrist & Patrinos (2018)

**Full cite (APA 7):** Altinok, N., Angrist, N., & Patrinos, H. A. (2018). *Global data set on education quality (1965–2015)*. World Bank Policy Research Working Paper No. 8314.

## Argument
Cross-country, cross-time test-score data are not directly comparable because they come from distinct international assessments (PISA, TIMSS, PIRLS, SACMEQ, PASEC, etc.) administered at different ages, on different subjects, on different scales. The paper presents a methodology to **harmonize** these into a single comparable Harmonized Learning Outcome (HLO) score covering ~163 countries from 1965 to 2015.

## Method
- Anchor international assessments to a common scale via overlapping country participations
- Adjust for cycle, subject, and grade differences
- Produce a single HLO value per country per assessment year
