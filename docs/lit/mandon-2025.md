# Lit Note: Mandon (2025)

**Full cite (APA 7):** Mandon, P. J.-C. (2025, February). *Beyond the AI divide: A simple approach to identifying global and local overperformers in AI preparedness* (Policy Research Working Paper No. 11073). World Bank.
**DOI / URL:** https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099517502242572646

## Argument
Constructs a methodology for identifying *overperformers* in AI preparedness — countries whose actual AI readiness score (per the IMF's 2023 Artificial Intelligence Preparedness Index) exceeds what their economic complexity (Economic Complexity Index) would predict. The analysis surfaces global and local overperformers by comparing fitted-vs-actual AI readiness, with case studies in Singapore, Northern Europe, Malaysia, Kazakhstan, Ghana, Rwanda, China, and India. Findings highlight the differential roles of regulation/ethics frameworks, digital infrastructure, and human capital across income levels.

## Method
- Cross-country positioning using IMF AI Preparedness Index (2023) + Economic Complexity Index
- Residual analysis (actual − predicted) to identify overperformers
- Case-study analysis of named countries against the global pattern

## Our engagement
**Practitioner antecedent for §4.6 joint composite — *methodologically distinct*.** Mandon uses the **IMF AI Preparedness Index** as the AI-readiness measure and constructs a *residual-from-economic-complexity* composite; our §4.6 uses the **Oxford Insights Government AI Readiness Index (GARI) 2025** and constructs a *multiplicative HCI × GARI/100* composite with a median-split quadrant analysis. The composites measure related-but-different things and answer related-but-different questions (Mandon: who is over-performing relative to economic-complexity expectations; ours: who is double-excluded on the joint distribution).

Cite at §1 (Introduction, ¶5) and §2 strand (c) Lit Review as the closest practitioner-side antecedent in the AI-readiness composite construction literature; our §4.6 is positioned as complementary, not as a duplication.

## Key claims to engage or cite
- AI-readiness composites are an active practitioner research area at the World Bank
- The over-performer framing (actual vs predicted by economic structure) is one valid approach; the joint-distribution framing we use is another
- The IMF AI Preparedness Index and Oxford GARI are alternative measures; choice between them is itself a measurement choice worth flagging
