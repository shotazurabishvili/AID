## Decisions made

- **Lock ADR-0005:** primary treatment column is `crs_disburse_usd_defl_ma3_lag1` (disburse × constant USD × 3-yr strictly-past MA, mean of t-3,t-2,t-1). Robustness specs: trailing-inclusive MA (Session-14 working spec), raw, commitment-side strictly-past MA. Full 16-cell sensitivity grid reported as a table; surface is sign-stable (all β ≥ 0 on HLO).
- **Why strictly-past over trailing-inclusive:** trailing-inclusive MA includes year-t disbursement on RHS of a year-t HLO regression — admits contemporaneous reverse-causation (donor responsiveness to in-year learning shocks). Strictly-past MA forecloses this by construction. Empirical cost: β drops from 10.9 (p=0.003) to 8.17 (p=0.10); sign and magnitude band preserved. Identification cleanness > marginal-significance optics.
- **Why disburse over commit as primary:** disbursement reflects money that actually flowed; commitment is signed pledge that may not be honored within panel window. For an outcome regression, disbursement is the theoretically-relevant quantity. Commitment shown in robustness — notably commit × strictly-past MA × HLO shows β=11.9** (stronger than disburse), confirming the positive pattern is not artifact of disbursement encoding.
- **Why constant over current USD:** constant-USD (DAC-deflator-adjusted) removes secular inflation drift over 2000-2020. Empirical effect on β is <5% across the 8-cell USD-basis comparison; principled choice is constant.
- **Manuscript framing implication (Session 04+ task, not Session 03):** the pre-Session-03 CLAUDE.md framing "ODA does not predict learning" is no longer accurate post-grid. All 16 HLO cells show β ≥ 0; half significant at p<0.05; one third at p<0.01. Falsification standard from `brief.md` § Self-Review Protocol is met at static-FE specification across every encoding tested.

## What we tried that didn't work

- **Inline current-USD lag1 computation considered, rejected.** Initial plan had 14 of 16 cells using pre-computed panel columns and 2 using inline `dplyr::lag()` in the sensitivity driver. Created a reproducibility-audit asymmetry. Replaced with: pre-compute 6 columns (added 2 current-USD lag1 cols) so the driver only does feols, no inline data transformations.
- **Hausman test failed (RE not estimable) — same as Session 14.** `plm::plm(... model="random", effect="twoways")` errors with "model not estimable: 6 coefficient(s) (incl. intercept) to be estimated but only 3 time(s) in data". This is a structural T-limit on the strictly-past MA spec — same root cause as Session-14 baseline 2e. Documented in lock diagnostics; not a blocker. FE remains justified theoretically.
- **Considered a separate Hausman re-attempt on trailing-inclusive MA cell for the lock diagnostics file** — rejected. The Hausman failure is panel-structural (T ≤ 3 effective time periods on the HCI cycles), not encoding-specific. Running on a different cell wouldn't add evidence.

| Family   | Raw         | Lag1        | Trailing MA3 | Strictly-past MA3 (lock) |

| Commit   | −0.18 (0.95)| 2.99 (0.12) | 9.28** (0.034)| 11.9** (0.011)           |

**Diagnostics on lock spec (`output/tables/model2_fe_sensitivity_diagnostics.csv`):** Wooldridge F=0.29 (p=0.59, no AR(1)); Breusch-Pagan χ²=138 (p=0.004, heteroskedasticity → cluster-robust SE applied); max VIF = 1.57 (no multicollinearity). Hausman undefined (T_eff ≤ 3, same as Session 14).

**Manuscript-level finding:** the brief's falsification standard ("if within-country β > 0 significant, thesis fails") is met on the static-FE specification across the entire encoding grid — not a single-cell artifact. The lock spec is the strictest possible (strictly-past MA, no reverse-causation channel) and still returns β=8.17 with marginal p=0.10. Framing-side implications (manuscript reframe candidates a/b/c per §5.2.1) deferred to Session 04+.
