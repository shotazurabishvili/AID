## Decisions made

- **Lock ADR-0008 (Option 2 confirmed):** OECD CRS disbursement is the primary treatment (per ADR-0005 lock); GCDF reported as parallel robustness. Empirical: spec B (OECD + GCDF covariate) shifts OECD β from 8.17 → 8.06 — a 0.02 SD movement, well within the pre-specified ±1 SD criterion. GCDF own coefficient is null (β = −0.27, p = 0.74).
- **Reject spec C as headline.** The `log(1 + CRS + GCDF)` combined-treatment encoding returns β = −0.35 (versus spec A's +8.17). This is a known artifact: log-of-sum compresses signal when the two flows are at very different magnitudes ($96M CRS mean vs $3.3M GCDF mean in-panel). Spec B (separate log covariates) is the right way to assess GCDF's contribution; spec C is recorded but not used as headline. Documented in ADR-0008 + findings §5.2.2.
- **SSA-stratified results are uninformative on this panel.** N=52 with ≤4 HCI cycles × 13 SSA countries clearing all controls yields wide CIs (CRS SE=14.1). Recorded but not interpreted as contradicting pooled.
- **§6 Discussion narrative shifts.** Pre-session, the planned story was "non-DAC blind spot biases OECD-only coefficient." Post-session, the correct story is "the blind spot is real but not consequential for the within-country coefficient in this sample" — Chinese aid joins OECD CRS in showing no within-country effect at the strictly-past spec.

## What we tried that didn't work

- **Combined treatment (spec C) initially tried as a *headline* substitute.** Returned β = −0.35 vs spec A's +8.17 — looked like it might contradict the lock. Diagnosing: realized log(CRS + GCDF) ≠ log(CRS) when GCDF is much smaller; the sum-then-log compresses within-country variance differently than separate logs. Switched interpretation: spec C is a *methodological caution* (not a headline candidate), spec B (separate logs) is the correct lock-criterion test. Documented in both ADR-0008 and findings §5.2.2.
- **Considered adding a region column to `R/30_merge_panel.R`** for the SSA filter. Rejected: only one script needs it (R/54), and the same `countrycode::codelist` lookup is already used in `R/40_eda_audit.R`. Keeping panel scope unchanged (only the new GCDF column gets added) is cleaner.

## Results / findings

**HLO outcome, all-sample, N=143:**

| Spec | Coef | β | SE | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — OECD-only (Session-03 lock) | log(1+CRS) | **8.17** | 4.91 | 0.10 |
| B — OECD + GCDF covariate | log(1+CRS) | **8.06*** | 4.75 | 0.095 |
| B — OECD + GCDF covariate | log(1+GCDF) | −0.26 | 0.77 | 0.74 |
| C — Combined log-of-sum | log(1+CRS+GCDF) | −0.35 | 1.12 | 0.76 |
| D — GCDF-only | log(1+GCDF) | −0.27 | 0.77 | 0.72 |

**Lock criterion verification:** sign preserved (✓), magnitude shift 0.02 SD (✓, well within ±1 SD), GCDF own coefficient null (✓, supports Option 2). Lock unambiguous.

**Substantive read:** the OECD-CRS-only headline is robust to the non-DAC blind spot at static-FE specification. Chinese aid does not show its own within-country effect on HLO in this panel — even where most concentrated (SSA 47/48 countries, $5.61B). Joins OECD CRS in showing no detectable within-country effect at the cleanest-identification spec.
