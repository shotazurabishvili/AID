## Decisions made

- **Session 08 prose came in at ~1,465w vs ~1,400w target = 5% over.** Substantively close-to-target. Trajectory: cumulative ~11,955w; Session 09 trim ~955w + §5.6 inclusion decision (≈150w if elected) → potential ~1,105w trim. Larger than the original 640-790w trim band but still feasible — most of the trim can come from §4.2's substantive-density paragraphs (Session 04's 50%-over locked-encoding-stack paragraph is the obvious first target) and from §6 subsection compression at author-review.
- **§6.4 framing chose "include structural variables" over "act on them in any prescriptive direction"** — the chastened-recommendation framing that the author-review brainstorm settled on. This avoids the failure mode where path-c-hedged headline plus prescriptive donor-allocation guidance creates internal tension; the "include" framing is defensible because the §4.2 + §4.7 structural-determinant coefficients are stable across specifications even where the ODA coefficient sign-flips.
- **§7 ¶1 closing on "the methodological-reflection tradition taken seriously as a working discipline rather than as a closing-paragraph caveat"** is the strongest positive-contribution framing the manuscript has made. It explicitly positions the paper as belonging to the Sandefur/Vivalt/Deaton-Cartwright tradition operationally, not just rhetorically.
- **§6.2 absorbed the original §6 outline (c) compounding-vulnerable-monitoring hook** as a one-sentence research-monitoring recommendation rather than a separate §6.5 subsection. This honors the original outline's policy-recommendation breadth without inflating the §6 count to 5 subsections; the "monitoring outcomes as future GARI and HCI editions arrive" framing also keeps the §4.6 caveats intact (snapshot-not-trajectory) rather than recommending donors fund AI-readiness on the strength of a snapshot.
- **§7 ¶3 closing line "ten thousand words drawing" is intentional self-reference** — it both numerically pegs the manuscript's scope and announces the author's authorship-discipline pride in having drawn the map at that scale rather than overclaiming. Author-review at Session 09 should verify this lands as confident-not-defensive.
- **Did NOT split the §6 AI-readiness-assistance original-outline hook into its own §6.5 subsection** — folded as a sentence into §6.2 (research-monitoring framing, not funding-allocation framing). Reasoning: the §4.6 ¶3 explicit longitudinal-claim hedge means a funding-allocation recommendation would create the same internal tension a referee would catch; research-monitoring as the next-generation-research recommendation is the same idea in chastened form.
