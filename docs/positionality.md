## What the positionality statement should accomplish

1. **Establish epistemic authority** — practitioner has seen what the data hide
2. **Pre-empt the "advocacy dressed as research" critique** — by acknowledging the lens openly
3. **Justify methodological choices** — particularly the skepticism toward enrollment-based metrics, the WGI critique, and the choice of disbursement-over-commitment ODA measures
4. **Avoid apology** — the brief is explicit: this is a methodological asset, not a weakness

## Risks to manage

- **Conflict of interest framing.** Avoid disclosing current employer in a way that implies institutional endorsement
- **Over-claim of insider authority.** "Worked in the sector" is honest; "uniquely positioned" is not — let the analysis speak
- **Geographic specificity.** Naming countries may signal where critiques are aimed; weigh against generality of claims
